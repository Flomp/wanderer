<objective>
Make `rewriteStyleForProxy` the whole transform: every URL-bearing field in the style — tiles,
glyphs and sprite alike — resolves through the loopback proxy, and the function stops needing a
filesystem cache root at all (D-11, D-01). Update both map hosts' call sites to match and delete
the map-open cache warm the write-through proxy replaces.

Purpose: the style is where the online/offline duality is actually expressed. RESEARCH.md 2 shows
three forked concerns; only tiles are genuinely binary per tile, and glyphs/sprite were forked
"for no structural reason". Once the proxy serves them (Plan 04), the transform can emit one
uniform set of loopback URLs, which is what makes a first-run-offline map render labels instead of
nothing and what lets the map-open warm go away.

This plan deliberately leaves `TrailMap.offline` and `NavigationScreen.isOffline` in place —
Plans 06 and 07 delete those in parallel. Here the two hosts change only their call arguments, so
every intermediate commit stays analyzer-clean and behaviourally self-consistent.

Output: a cache-root-free unified transform, two updated call sites, and updated tests.
</objective>
