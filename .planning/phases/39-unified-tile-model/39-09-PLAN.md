<objective>
Remove the rejected pulse mechanism, then verify the whole phase on a physical device.

**Scope changed after Plan 01's risk gate.** This plan originally added a connectivity re-probe
loop (D-15) and fired a recovery mechanism on the regain edge (D-12). On-device testing rejected
that mechanism outright: the pulse fires correctly and MapLibre re-schedules nothing — 82
Connection-class tile failures before the pulse, **zero** tile requests after. The failures are
errored entries in the source's tile pyramid, not pending requests, so a connectivity edge has
nothing to act on. Full evidence in `39-01-SUMMARY.md` `## Risk gate outcome`.

The documented `setStyle` reload fallback was then declined as disproportionate — it rebuilds
every source, layer and image, dropping and re-adding the navigation screen's trail track and
breadcrumb on every connectivity regain. The developer accepted user-initiated recovery instead
(CONTEXT.md D-12a), which is sufficient because Plan 03's redirect-on-miss already kills the
reported defect on its own: with the old offline style every tile pointed at the proxy with no
upstream, so panning into uncovered area was blank forever. Now a pan succeeds.

D-15's re-probe loop went with it — it existed only to trigger the recovery. The map no longer
consults `onlineStatusProvider` at all, since D-03 makes the proxy connectivity-blind.

What remains: delete the dead code, and verify the phase on hardware.
</objective>
